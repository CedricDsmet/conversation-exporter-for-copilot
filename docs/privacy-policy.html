Privacy Policy

Conversation Exporter for Copilot · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Conversation Exporter for Copilot (“the extension”) is a browser extension published by Cedric De Smet. It saves the Microsoft Copilot conversation you have open as a file or copies it to your clipboard. This policy explains what the extension does with your data. The short version: it does not collect, store or send any of it.

What the extension accesses

When you click the extension's toolbar button and choose an export option, the extension reads the content of the conversation shown in your current tab. This includes your messages, Copilot's answers, and any images, tables, code or diagrams in them. It also reads the page title and address to name the file and add the source link to it.

It can only do this for the tab you are looking at, and only at the moment you click. It has no access to your other tabs, your browsing history or any other website.

What happens with that content

  • The conversation is converted to HTML or Markdown entirely inside your browser.
  • The result is saved to your own Downloads folder or copied to your clipboard, depending on what you choose.
  • To keep images visible in the exported file, the extension may download images that are already shown in the conversation again, from the same addresses the Copilot page itself uses. Nothing is sent along with those requests apart from what your browser normally sends to that site.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send your conversations or any other data to the publisher or to any third party.
  • It does not store conversations. Nothing is kept after the export is done.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
  • It does not use cookies and does not create an account.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it has none to sell or share.

Permissions

  • activeTab gives temporary access to the current tab, only after you click the extension.
  • scripting runs the bundled exporter script in that tab to read the conversation.

Your files

The exported files are yours. Where they go next, for example an email you attach them to, is up to you. Exported conversations may contain sensitive information from your chats, so treat them like any other document.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If the extension's handling of data ever changes, this policy will be updated and the date above will change. A change that involves collecting data would be announced in the store listing before it takes effect.
